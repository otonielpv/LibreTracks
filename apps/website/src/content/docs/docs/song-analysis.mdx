Preparing a song by hand takes a while: set the tempo, check the time
signature, find the key and listen to the guide to place each marker.
**Analyze song** does it for you from the multitrack's own audio:

- the **tempo and time signature**, read from the **click** track;
- the song's **key**, and its **key changes** if it modulates, from the tracks
  with harmony;
- the **section markers** (Intro, Verse, Chorus…), by listening to what the
  **voice guide** says.

It changes nothing without asking: it shows you a proposal, you review it and
choose what to apply. And it can be undone.

## Opening it

Right-click the song (long-press on mobile) ›
**Analyze song (tempo, key and sections)…**. It works on the computer, on
Android and on iOS.

![The Analyze song window before starting](/guide/desktop-en/song-analysis-setup.png)

<Legend>

1. **Click track**: where the tempo comes from. LibreTracks picks the track by
   its name; change it if it is not the click. If the song has no click,
   choose **No click: estimate it from the music**, or **Don't analyze the
   tempo**.
2. **Analyze the key**: the tracks listened to for the key. The click, the
   guide and the percussion are already left out, since they blur the key.
3. **Guide track**: the voice that announces the sections. **Don't analyze the
   guide** leaves the markers as they are.
4. **Analyze**: starts. It takes a few seconds per song (a bit more if it
   recognises the guide) and shows the progress.

</Legend>

The first time you analyze a guide, the window offers to download the
**speech recognition** (about 57 MB, only once). With it, announcements in
**any voice** are understood, male or female, in Spanish or English. Without
it, only guides recorded with the same voice the app uses are recognised.

:::caution[Don't run it mid-show]
The analysis uses a fair amount of CPU for a few seconds. Do it while
preparing the session, not while you play.
:::

## Reviewing the proposal

When it finishes, the window shows what it found, part by part. Each part has
its **Apply** box: untick what you don't want.

![The proposal: tempo and meter, key and markers](/guide/desktop-en/song-analysis-review-1.png)

**Tempo and meter.** The tempo and time signature the song has and the ones it
suggests, and a table with each tempo and time signature marker, which you can
edit. Click a position to jump there and hear it. If the click plays eighth
notes and the tempo came out double (or half), the **×2** and **÷2** buttons
fix it. An orange warning tells you when to check it, for example when the
click does not accent beat 1.

**Key.** The song's key and, if it modulates, each **key change** with its
moment. You can change any of them before applying. The changes then show as
a notch on the song's band: see [Key changes](/docs/tasks/songs/#key-changes).

**Section markers.** One row for each announcement the guide makes, with what
it understood in quotes:

![The guide's announcements and each one's section](/guide/desktop-en/song-analysis-review-3.png)

- **▶** plays the announcement so you can hear it.
- **Section** is the marker that gets placed (Intro, Verse, Chorus…) and
  **Cue** an extra hint, like *Drums In*. Announcements repeated with the same
  recording are grouped: choose once what it is and it applies to every time
  it is said.
- **The guide announces**: how many bars before the section the guide speaks.
  The usual is **1 bar before**; change it if your markers come out shifted.
- If the song already had markers, the proposal **replaces** them; untick the
  box to keep yours.

Press **Apply** to write it into the song, or **Back** to change the tracks
and try again. What you apply is undone with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>.

## Analyzing every song

For a whole session, **File › Analyze all songs…** (on mobile, in the
File sheet of the side rail) analyzes each song one after another. Each one
uses the tracks its own analysis would pick by name (Click, Guide…); tick or
untick the songs you want and press **Analyze**. It takes a few seconds per
song and uses a fair amount of CPU: better not to run it during a show.

![The Analyze all songs window](/guide/desktop-en/song-analysis-batch.png)

1. **All songs** ticks or unticks the whole list.
2. Each song with what will be read from it (click, music, key, guide).
3. **Analyze** starts, one song after another.

When it finishes you have two ways forward:

- **Review** opens that song's proposal in the usual window, so you can adjust
  it before applying it.
- **Apply all** writes every proposal as it is. Markers a song already had are
  kept; to replace them with the guide's, review that song.

Each song is applied as its own step, so it undoes on its own.

## Tips

- **Check the tempo before the markers.** If the sections land mid-bar or
  shifted, the tempo almost always came out double or half: fix it with ×2 /
  ÷2 before applying.
- **It works with warp and with the song transposed.** The audio is analyzed
  as recorded, and the markers land in their musical place.
- **Click and guide on the same track.** If the click and the voice guide come
  together (for example after
  [splitting a file with the click on one side](/docs/tasks/clips-tracks/#a-file-with-the-click-on-one-side)),
  choose that track as the **Click track** and as the **Guide track**.
  LibreTracks separates the click hits from the voice and reads each one on
  its own. A track in *Left only* or *Right only* is analyzed as it sounds,
  with its channel.
- **Go over the proposal once.** The analysis gets most multitracks right, but
  a guide with a lot of background noise or a live click that follows the band
  can throw it off. That is what the review is for.
