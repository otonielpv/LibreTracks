LibreTracks can **change the lyrics in your presentation program** in time
with the song: when the Chorus comes, the lyrics program shows the Chorus. It
talks to **ProPresenter**, **FreeShow** and **Holyrics** directly over the
network: no virtual MIDI ports, no mapping notes to slides and nothing to
program song by song.

It works with the **section markers** you already have (Verse 1, Chorus,
Bridge…): each one is matched by name with the group of the same name in the
presentation, and LibreTracks sends it **a little before** the section starts,
so the words are on screen when the singing begins. It follows live jumps,
Vamp and arrangements like the rest of the show.

## 1. Turning it on

Open **Settings › Lyrics**, turn on **Send the lyrics to the presentation
program** and pick your program. The first time, a **step-by-step tutorial**
shows how to enable the network in that program. If you already know how,
press **Skip tutorial**; you can see it again any time with **Show the
tutorial**.

### ProPresenter

1. In ProPresenter open **Settings** (Preferences on Mac) › **Network**.
2. Tick **Enable Network**. You do not need ProPresenter Remote or a password.
3. Copy the **port** it shows (for example 50001) into LibreTracks.

### FreeShow

1. In FreeShow open **Settings › Connections**.
2. Enable the **REST API** (port 5506 by default).
3. If you set a key, copy it into LibreTracks' **Key** field.

### Holyrics

1. In Holyrics: **File › Settings › API Server**.
2. Tick **API server - Local** (port 8091). It is free: the paid plan is only
   for the API over the internet.
3. Press **Manage Permissions**, create a **token** and tick in the **Local**
   column: GetSongs, GetLyrics, ShowLyrics, ActionGoToIndex, ActionNext, SetF8,
   GetCurrentPresentation and GetSlideDescriptions. Tick SetF10 too (**Clear
   the screen** to black) and SetF9 (**Clear the text**, keeping the
   background); without them F8, Holyrics' wallpaper, is used.
4. Paste the token into LibreTracks. It is stored in the system keychain,
   never in a file.

If the program runs **on another computer**, type its IP in **Computer**. On
the same one, leave `127.0.0.1`. Press **Test connection**: you see the
version and how many songs it found, or what is wrong in plain words (and, in
Holyrics, the exact permissions missing).

If you do not know the IP, press **Search the network**: LibreTracks looks for
the chosen program on the computers on your network and lists it with its
port; tap one to fill in Computer and Port. On iPhone or iPad, the first time
you have to allow **Local Network** access.

## 2. Linking the songs

Each song links **on its own** to the presentation with **the same name**
(ignoring accents, case, "(Live)", the track number or a key at the end). To
check, press the **Lyrics** pill in the top bar › **Link songs…**:

- ✔ **linked**: done.
- ? **suggestion**: press **Use «…»** or pick another from the list. **Accept
  all suggestions** does them all at once.
- ✗ **not found**: search for it by name.

The link is stored **by name**: a song exported as `.ltpkg` keeps working at
another church on another computer.

## 3. How sections are matched

In the song's menu, **Lyrics on screen…** shows each marker and the group it
will send:

- **Verse 2** prefers the group **Verse 2** and, if there is none, falls back
  to **Verse**.
- **Chorus** matches **Chorus** and **Chorus 1**; **Refrain** too.
- Sections that are played, not sung (**intro**, **instrumental**,
  **interlude**, **vamp**, **solo**, **outro**, **ending**…) with no group in
  the presentation **clear the text** by themselves: the lyrics go and the
  background stays. If the presentation has a group for them, it is shown.
- A marker that matches nothing shows in amber and **nothing is sent** (the
  screen stays as it is).

To correct it, pick another group in its list, **Clear the screen** (no text,
no background), **Clear the text** (takes the lyrics away and keeps the
background: handy for interludes and instrumentals) or **Do not change**. If the section repeats, LibreTracks asks whether the change is
**only this time** or **every time**. **Test** sends that section right away,
without playing.

Repeated sections (the choruses of an arrangement) go to their own
appearance: the song's third chorus goes to the presentation's third chorus,
if there is one.

## 4. Slides of each section

In the **Slides** column of the song panel, **Edit** opens that marker's
editor. Each repeat is edited on its own (the second bridge leaves the first
alone).

- **Slides of this marker**: each one shows its text and, in FreeShow and
  ProPresenter, its real thumbnail (they fill in over a few seconds). Tap the
  ones that belong to it. By default they
  are its group's; if your presentation repeats the bridge four times in a
  row, LibreTracks sees one bridge of four slides. **One part per repeat**
  splits them among the times the section comes round: each bridge gets its
  own. **Back to the group's slides** undoes the choice.
- **When it moves to the next one**: **Automatic** (spread over whole bars),
  **Every N bars**, **Recorded** or **Only the first**.
- **Record this section**: playback starts one bar before the marker; tap
  **Change** (or the space bar) on each slide change. Each tap is sent at once
  so you see it work, and recording stops by itself when the section ends.
  **Save recording** sets the section to **Recorded**. It is stored in beats,
  so it follows the tempo.

## When the song starts

If the song does not start right on a marker, **When the song starts**
decides what shows until the first one: **Automatic** shows the
presentation's title slide if it has one and a blank screen otherwise (so the
previous song's lyrics do not stay up). You can also pick **Clear the
screen** or any slide.

## 5. Live

The **Lyrics** pill in the top bar tells you how it goes:

- **green**: connected;
- **amber**: connected, but some songs have no presentation;
- **red**: not connected or no permissions;
- **grey**: sending paused.

Press it to see the last send ("Chorus → Chorus, 18 ms"), **Pause sending**
(for example when the projection operator wants to drive it by hand) and
**Refresh** after changing presentations in the program.

When a song ends the screen is cleared (turn it off in Settings › Lyrics if
you prefer it to stay). Pausing never clears.

With **redundancy**, only the main sends; the backup takes over if the main
is lost, without sending anything twice.

## Tips

- Use the **Cut** transition in the lyrics program: a long transition makes
  the lyrics arrive late.
- With two computers, a **cable** is better; on Wi-Fi, use 5 GHz.
- The default **lead** is **1 beat**. Change it in Settings › Lyrics, or for
  one song in its panel.

## Common problems

- **It does not connect**: check that the program is open with its network
  enabled, the IP and port (ProPresenter picks its own port) and that the
  Windows firewall is not blocking it.
- **Missing permissions** (Holyrics): tick the permissions Test connection
  lists in Manage Permissions, Local column.
- **It jumps to the first chorus**: the program has a single "Chorus" group;
  that is fine, the text is the same. Otherwise name the groups like your
  markers.
- **It arrives late**: raise the lead and use the Cut transition.
