A LibreTracks session is a **setlist**: several songs one after another on the
timeline. Each song is a named bar on the ruler (in some places the app calls
it a *region*) and holds its clips, its markers and its tempo changes.
Everything inside a song travels with it.

## Creating a song

There are several ways, depending on where the audio comes from:

- **From a Library folder**: drag the folder onto the timeline. See
  [Library and audio](/docs/tasks/library/#a-whole-folder-as-a-song).
- **Dropping audio into an empty gap** on the timeline: a song is created to
  hold it.
- **From a stretch of the ruler**: drag across an empty part of the ruler to
  mark the stretch, right-click inside it and choose **Create Song From
  Selection**.

  caption="Dragging across the empty ruler marks a stretch; right-click › Create Song From Selection."

- **Importing an exported song** (`.ltpkg`): File menu › **Import song**, or
  drag the file onto the timeline. It arrives with its audio, its markers and
  its settings, and is placed at the end of the session.
- **Importing a Reaper or Ableton project** (`.rpp`, `.als`). See
  [Export, import and share](/docs/integration-ecosystem/).
- In **Compact View**, the **+ New song** button creates an empty song at the
  end.

## Moving, lengthening and shortening

**Move**: drag the song's bar along the ruler. Its clips, markers and tempo
changes move with it.

<Clip
  src="/guide/desktop-en/song-move.mp4"
  poster="/guide/desktop-en/song-move.webp"
  caption="Dragging a song's bar moves it with everything in it."
/>

- To the **right**, if it hits the next song it **pushes** it, and every song
  after it too.
- To the **left** it cannot overlap the previous one.
- It snaps to the grid; hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to move it freely.

**Lengthen or shorten**: drag the bar's left or right edge.

<Clip
  src="/guide/desktop-en/song-resize.mp4"
  poster="/guide/desktop-en/song-resize.webp"
  caption="Dragging the song's right edge lengthens it."
/>

- The **right edge** snaps to the beat; the **left** one, to the bar.
- An edge **cannot cross the audio** the song holds: it stops right at the
  first or last clip. That way a clip is never left outside.
- Nor can it run into the song next to it.
- Lengthening the end leaves silence after the last clip, handy for giving
  some air before the next song.

## Splitting a song in two

Handy when you import a whole project as a single song and want to split it
into the setlist's real songs.

1. Put the cursor where you want to cut.
2. Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, or right-click the song › **Split song
   at cursor**.

The left part keeps the name; the right one gets the same name with "(2)".
Both keep the original's settings (key, warp, master). Clips that cross the
cut are split too, without losing any audio.

## Each song's settings

Each song has its own settings. Select it by clicking its bar to see them in
the [toolbar](/docs/interface/toolbar/#master-transposition-and-warp), or use
its menu (right-click):

- **Rename Song** (<kbd>F2</kbd>).
- **Song key**: its key. It shows next to the name, in Live View and on the
  Remote, and **transposes by itself** when you change the pitch: a song in
  *D* raised 2 semitones shows as *E*.
- **Master**: the volume of the whole song. It does not affect the metronome
  or the voice guide.
- **Transposition** and **warp**: see
  [Pitch, warp and the T button](/docs/pitch-and-warp/). Note: without warp,
  transposing changes the speed, so the song gets longer or shorter, and the
  following ones shift so they don't overlap.
- **Change Original BPM**: the tempo the audio was recorded at, used by warp.

## Reordering the setlist

- In the **DAW view**, move the songs along the ruler (see above).
- In **Compact View** and **Live View**, drag the song by its **handle** (the
  six dots on its left) to its new place. While you drag you see where it will
  land. With the handle selected, the arrow keys move it one place at a time.
  The song moves whole, with its audio, markers and tempo, and the gaps
  between songs are kept.

## Going from one song to the next live

By default, when a song ends playback carries on with the next one. If you
would rather stop between songs (to speak, for example), turn on **Pause at
the end of each song** in Settings › General: playback stops right on the last
frame of each song, and *Play* starts the next one.

To jump to another song: the **Previous** and **Next** buttons,
<kbd>Shift</kbd>+number (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> is the first song),
Live View, the Remote or a MIDI pedal. When and how the jump happens is set by
[Song Transition](/docs/interface/toolbar/#song-transition).

## Exporting, rendering and deleting

From the song's menu:

- **Export Song**: creates an `.ltpkg` with the song, its audio, its markers
  and its settings, to take it to another session or another computer.
- **Render audio…**: exports the mix to WAV, whole or one track per file.
- **Delete Song** (<kbd>Del</kbd> with the song selected): removes the song
  with its clips, markers and tempo changes. If it still has clips, it asks
  you to confirm. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes it, and the audio stays
  in the Library.
