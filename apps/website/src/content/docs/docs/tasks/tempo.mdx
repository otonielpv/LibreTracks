Tempo and time signature don't move the audio: what they do is tell
LibreTracks **where the bars and beats fall**. The grid and snap, the numbers
on the ruler, the **metronome**, the **voice guide**'s count, *after X bars*
jumps and Vamp by bars all depend on them. If the metronome doesn't line up
with the music, the tempo is what needs fixing.

Each song has its own. When you import a song, it arrives with its tempo and
time signature already set at its start.

:::note[What if I want the music to go faster?]
Changing the BPM doesn't speed up the audio. That is what **warp** is for:
with the song's warp on, the audio stretches to follow the timeline's tempo
without changing pitch. See [Pitch, warp and the T button](/docs/pitch-and-warp/).
:::

## Changing the tempo of a section

In the top bar:

- **BPM**: type the tempo and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. It changes the tempo
  section the cursor is in; the *Tempo @ 6:12.4* label tells you where that
  section starts. It accepts decimals (for example, 152.8).
- **Tap**: tap along with the music. From the second tap it works out the
  tempo from the average of the last eight, and applies it to the same
  section. If more than two and a half seconds go by without a tap, the count
  starts again.

Neither of them creates a new tempo change: they correct the one that is
already there.

## Changing tempo mid-song

1. **Right-click the ruler**, at the point where the tempo changes.
2. Choose **Change Timeline BPM**.
3. Type the new tempo.

  caption="Right-click the ruler › Change Timeline BPM: from that point, the song plays at 132."

A turquoise marker with the BPM appears. From there to the next change,
everything plays at that tempo. If you do the same at the start of the
session, you change the starting tempo instead of creating a marker.

Right-click the marker: **Change BPM** or **Delete Marker** (the section goes
back to the previous tempo). On mobile, the action bar › **…** › **Tempo**
creates the change at the cursor position.

## Changing time signature

Same as the tempo: right-click the ruler › **Create time-signature marker**,
and type the metre (for example, `6/8` or `3/4`).

<Clip
  src="/guide/desktop-en/time-signature-change.mp4"
  poster="/guide/desktop-en/time-signature-change.webp"
  caption="Right-click the ruler › Create time-signature marker › 6/8: the ruler is renumbered from that point."
/>

- The numerator goes from 1 to 32 and the denominator can be 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or
  32.
- The **Time signature** field in the top bar works differently from the BPM:
  with the cursor at the start of the session it changes the starting time
  signature, and anywhere else it **creates a time-signature change** at the
  cursor position.
- Right-click the time-signature marker: **Change time signature** or
  **Delete Marker**.

The voice guide counts according to the time signature: in 3/4 it says
"1, 2, 3", in 5/4 it counts up to five.

## Checking the tempo is right

1. Turn on the **metronome** (the *Click* button).
2. Play the song: the click has to land with the drums.
3. If it gradually drifts ahead or behind, the tempo is wrong: use *Tap* or
   type the exact value.
4. If it keeps time but is shifted, the song doesn't start on a downbeat.
   Move the song (or the audio) until the first hit lands at the start of a
   bar.
