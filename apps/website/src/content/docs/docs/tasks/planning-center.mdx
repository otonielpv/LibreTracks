If your church keeps its songs in **Planning Center Services**, LibreTracks can
bring their lyrics and chords into each song's
[lyrics and chords](/docs/live-view/#lyrics-and-chords). Nothing to export and
no text to copy: search the song, pick the arrangement, check and done.

What matters is that **the lyrics follow your song, not the sheet**. Planning
Center may write Intro, Verse, Chorus; if your song goes Intro, Verse, Verse,
Chorus, Live shows Intro, Verse, Verse, Chorus.

## 1. Connect the account

Open the **Cloud** panel (the Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox accounts).
**Planning Center** is at the bottom: press **Connect with Planning Center**,
sign in in the browser and come back to the app. You will see your name and
your organization.

- The account is yours: LibreTracks **reads** songs, arrangements and plans,
  and only writes when you press **Save to Planning Center**.
- The access is kept in the system credential store, not in the session or
  any file.
- If your user has no access to **Services** in the organization, the app says
  so: ask whoever runs Planning Center.
- **Disconnect** removes the access from this device.

## 2. Import the lyrics of a song

1. Open the song's lyrics editor (song menu, **Lyrics and chords**), or in
   the song menu **Lyrics from Planning Center…**.
2. Press **From Planning Center…**. The search already has the song's name.
3. Pick the song, then the arrangement. An arrangement with only a PDF shows
   as **PDF only** and cannot be picked: there is no text to bring.
4. The sheet arrives in the editor **as it is in Planning Center**, untouched.
   **Adjust to the sequence** opens so you see what it would change; apply it,
   or cancel and keep the sheet as it is.

### Adjust to the sequence

It works the same for Planning Center, a PDF, text or ChordPro: it is in the
lyrics editor and changes nothing until you press **Apply**.

- **Changes in the sheet**: what will be removed or moved, one by one (the
  `// … //` repeat slashes, an empty "CHORUS" repeating one already written,
  an identical copy, chords written on the header, page marks).
- **The song marker by marker**, in playing order, with the sheet section each
  one will show, its first line and how many times it plays inside the marker
  (**×2**: a long chorus sung twice). Doubtful markers say **Check** or
  **Unsure**.
- When you change a marker's section it stays as you left it and the rest is
  fitted again around it. The **×N** can be changed too.
- If the sheet is in another key, the **transpose** box moves the chords to
  your song's key.
- Without section markers, **Apply without linking**, or create them first
  with [Analyse song](/docs/song-analysis/).

## 3. How sections are matched

The song's markers lead: they sit on the audio. For each marker LibreTracks
looks for the sheet section that belongs there:

- **Different names for the same part**: Chorus, Coro, Estribillo, Refrão and
  Refrain are the same part; so are Interlude, Instrumental and Turnaround; and
  Ending, Outro and Coda. It reads English, Spanish and Portuguese, Roman
  numerals ("Verse II") and decorations like "Chorus (x2)" or "Final chorus".
- **Numbers**: a **Verse 2** marker prefers the **Verse 2** section. A marker
  without a number reads the sheet in order.
- **Repeats**: if the sheet writes the chorus once and the song sings it three
  times, all three markers show that chorus.
- **The Planning Center sequence** tells which of two verses is which when your
  markers only say "Verse".
- **Parts without words**: an intro or an instrumental with no section in the
  sheet stay without lyrics, silently. A sung marker without a section warns.
- Planning Center's short forms (V1, C, PC, B) are read in its sequence, never
  in a marker's name: a marker called "C" is not a chorus.

| Your marker | Matches in the sheet |
| --- | --- |
| Chorus, Refrain, Estribillo | Chorus, Coro, Refrão |
| Interlude, Instrumental | Interlude, Instrumental, Turnaround |
| Ending, Outro | Ending, Outro, Coda |
| Pre-Chorus | Pre-Chorus, Pre-Coro |
| Bridge | Bridge, Puente, Ponte |

The same matching is used when importing a PDF or text.

## 4. Update from Planning Center

On an imported song, the lyrics editor offers **Update from Planning Center**.
If the arrangement has not changed, it says so. If it has, you see which
sections were **added**, **removed** or **changed**; **Bring to the editor**
puts the new sheet as it comes and opens **Adjust to the sequence** with each
marker keeping its section.

If you edited the lyrics in LibreTracks after importing them, a warning tells
you those changes will be lost. It never updates on its own.

## 5. Import a whole plan

To get Sunday's service ready at once: in the Planning Center row of the Cloud
panel, **Import a plan…**.

1. Pick the service type and one of the upcoming plans.
2. Each song of the plan is paired with a song of the session by title ("(Live)"
   or a key in the name do not matter). Fix a pair in its menu, or leave it as
   **Not in the session**.
3. **Prepare** reads each arrangement, with the sequence and key the plan says.
   You can **Stop** at any time.
4. **Apply the clear ones and review the rest** saves the songs where
   everything fits for sure and opens the review of the others, one by one. Or
   **Review one by one** all of them.

Each song is saved whole or not at all: stopping half way leaves no half
lyrics.

## 6. Save to Planning Center

Once the sheet is fixed and adjusted to your song, **Save to Planning Center**
(in the lyrics editor) sends it back with your song's sequence, so nobody has
to fix it there by hand.

- By default it creates a **new arrangement** in the song (with the name you
  choose): the original stays as it is for everyone.
- **Replace the arrangement** is a separate choice: it shows what changes,
  warns if the arrangement changed in Planning Center since you imported it,
  and asks to confirm, because everyone using it will see it.
- You need permission to edit songs in your organization.
- It never saves on its own: only with the button.

## Common problems

- **Planning Center does not show**: this version of the app does not include
  it yet.
- **"Your user has no access to Services"**: ask for Services access in your
  Planning Center organization.
- **A verse lands in the wrong place**: change it in the review. If your
  markers say "Verse 1" and "Verse 2", the matching is surer.
- **"PDF only"**: that arrangement has no lyrics as text in Planning Center.
  Import the PDF with **Import file…** in the editor.
