Each song's lyrics are written once and LibreTracks shows them in Live,
Setlist, full screen and the remote, following the song as it plays. You do
not need to know ChordPro.

## Open the editor

In the lyrics panel, if the song has no lyrics yet, press **Write lyrics**. If
it has, press the pencil in the panel's toolbar.

The editor has two modes at the top: **Simple** and **Advanced (ChordPro)**.
Both edit the same lyrics; switch between them without losing anything. The
app remembers the last one you used.

## Simple mode: part by part

On the left are the **sections of the song**, one per marker in the order they
play, as on the timeline (01 Intro, 02 Verse, 03 Chorus…). Press one and write
its lyrics on the right.

- A ✓ means the section already has lyrics.
- Sections with the same name **share their lyrics**: write the Chorus once
  and it counts for every Chorus. A link icon marks them and, above the box,
  you see where else it plays.
- Parts that usually have no lyrics (intro, instrumental) look dimmer, but you
  can give them chords.
- **Alt + ↑ / ↓** changes part and **Ctrl + Enter** saves.

### Chords over the lyrics

Write "like on paper": chords on the line above, lined up with spaces over the
syllable they fall on.

```
G              D
Holy is the Lord
```

If you prefer ChordPro (`[G]Holy is the [D]Lord`), it works in the box too.
Blank lines are ignored.

On a tablet you can also use **Chords by touch**: tap a word to give it a
chord (note, type and bass, with the song's chords first) and tap a chord to
change or remove it.

### When one time has different lyrics

If the last chorus says something else, give its marker another name (for
example "Final chorus"): it gets its own lyrics. In Advanced mode you can also
choose which section each marker shows.

### Songs without markers

Without section markers, add parts with the quick buttons (**Add Verse**,
**Add Chorus**…), which number themselves. Live shows the whole lyrics, but
to **follow** the song it needs section markers.

## Paste the whole song

Press **Paste whole song** and paste the lyrics as you have them. With headers
(CHORUS, Verse 2, [Bridge]) they are split by them; without, by blank lines,
as in Holyrics. The block that repeats is proposed as the Chorus.

Before applying you see each block with the part it was given: change it if
needed and press **Apply**. Nothing is saved without that review.

## Import

**Import…** opens a file (PDF, ChordPro, text) or, with your account connected,
[Planning Center](/docs/tasks/planning-center/). What you import is fitted to
the sequence and comes back to the editor already split into parts. Planning
Center sheets can be edited like any other: the app still knows they were
edited here.

## Advanced mode (ChordPro)

The editor as always: the full ChordPro text, which section each marker shows,
recorded line times, passes and **Recalculate times**.

## Lyrics follow

In the lyrics panel's toolbar, the **Follow** button chooses what is
highlighted as "what is sung now" and what stays in view:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **1 line** | The current line, near the top (default). |
| **2 lines** / **3 lines** | Fixed groups of the part (1-2, 3-4…); the lyrics only move when the group changes. |
| **Whole section** | The whole current part; its title goes to the top when it starts. If it does not fit, it turns pages. |
| **No follow** | The line is highlighted but nothing moves. |

- **In all songs** is this device's choice.
- **Only in this song** is saved with the song (it travels with the session
  and packages) and wins over the general one. **Like all songs** removes it.
- If you scroll the lyrics by hand, the follow waits a few seconds and comes
  back on its own.
- In the **remote**, the lyrics widget has its own follow button; if the song
  has its own, the song's wins.

## On a phone

On a phone the editor opens in portrait and the parts show as chips above the
box. On a tablet, in landscape, the list stays on the left as on a computer.
