Below the toolbar is the timeline: the **ruler** on top, the **track headers**
on the left and the **clips** on the right. Almost everything you edit here is
done with a **right-click**, which opens a different menu depending on where
you click.

## The ruler

![The ruler with a song, both kinds of marker and a tempo change](/guide/desktop-en/ruler.png)

<Legend>

1. **Song**: each named bar is a song in the session (the app also calls them
   *regions*). Click to select it and drag to move it. Drag it past the middle
   of another song and they swap places: the others rearrange to show you the
   new order, and it is applied when you let go. **Move earlier** and **Move
   later**, in its menu (right-click; long-press on mobile), do the same one
   step at a time. The letter to the right
   of the name, if there is one, is its key.
2. **Cue markers** (top row): *Drums In*, *Last Time*… They are not jump
   destinations. The [voice guide](/docs/voice-guide/) announces them and the
   [Remote](/docs/remote-control/) shows them to the band.
3. **Section markers** (bottom row): *Intro*, *Verse*, *Chorus*… Click one to
   jump to it. When the jump happens is set by
   [Marker Jump](/docs/interface/toolbar/#marker-jump).
4. **Tempo change**: from that point the song plays at another BPM.
   Time-signature changes look the same, with the time signature instead of the
   BPM.

</Legend>

The numbers on the ruler are the position in bars (*1.1.00*) and, below, the
elapsed time. Click the ruler, away from the markers, to move the cursor.

## Track header

![The controls of an audio track header](/guide/desktop-en/track-header.png)

<Legend>

1. Track **name**. Select it and press <kbd>F2</kbd> to rename it.
2. **M** (mute): silences the track.
3. **S** (solo): plays only this track and any others in solo.
4. **Volume** in decibels. 0 dB is the audio's original volume. Double-click
   the fader to go back to 0 dB.
5. **Audio output**: where the track plays. *Master* is the main output;
   *Out 3/4*, *Out 5*… are physical outputs of your interface;
   *Inherited (Folder)* uses the output of the folder it is in. See
   [Audio outputs and metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/).
6. **T** (transposition): with the song's warp on, it decides whether this
   track changes key when you transpose. Turn it off on the click or the guide
   so they do not go out of tune. See [the T button](/docs/pitch-and-warp/).
7. **Pan**: left/right. *C* is center; double-click to go back to the center.
8. Track level **meter**, with one bar per channel: left and right. See
   [below](#the-meter).

</Legend>

With several tracks selected (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click on
the headers), touching any of these controls changes it on all of them. Volume
and pan move by the same proportion on all of them, so the balance of the mix
is kept.

### The meter

![A track's stereo meter while it plays](/guide/desktop-en/track-meter-stereo.png)

The meter has **two bars**, one per channel, so you see at a glance where the
track sits: with the pan to the left, the left bar rises higher. If the signal
clips, the top of **the channel that clips** lights up red, and a small white
line holds the highest peak for a moment.

If you make the track **mono**, **left only** or **right only** (right-click
the track › *Channels…*), the meter becomes **a single bar**, and goes back to
two with *Stereo*. The
same goes for the mixer of the [Compact View](/docs/compact-view/).

To change a track's height, drag its bottom edge. With <kbd>Alt</kbd> you change
all of them at once, and a double-click goes back to the normal height.

### Folders

![A folder header](/guide/desktop-en/folder-header.png)

<Legend>

1. **Open or close** the folder (**+** / **−**). Closed, you see a single block
   with the number of tracks it holds.
2. **Folder output**: the tracks inside set to *Inherited (Folder)* go out
   here. You change the output of the whole drum kit, for example, in one
   place.

</Legend>

The folder's **M**, **S** and volume controls affect all of its tracks.

## Right-click menus

### On a song

![A song's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-song.png)

- **Rename Song** (<kbd>F2</kbd>).
- **Song key**: its key. It shows next to the name and is transposed with it.
- **Key change at cursor ▸**: the song changes key from where the cursor is
  (the last chorus a step up, for example). See
  [Key changes](/docs/tasks/songs/#key-changes).
- **Arrangement**: opens the [arrangement](/docs/arrangements/) editor.
- **Split song at cursor** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>): splits it into two
  songs at the cursor position.
- **Change Original BPM**: the tempo the audio was recorded at, used by
  [warp](/docs/pitch-and-warp/).
- **Song Fades ▸**: the song's fade in and fade out, in seconds. You can also
  set them by dragging the corners of the song in the ruler. See
  [Fade in and fade out](/docs/tasks/songs/#fade-in-and-fade-out).
- **Voice Guide Count-in ▸**: how the voice guide counts this song in, if you
  want it different from the global setting. See
  [Voice guide](/docs/voice-guide/#turning-it-on-and-setting-it-up).
- **Export Song**: saves it as an `.ltpkg` to take to another session.
- **Render audio…**: exports its mix to WAV, whole or track by track.
- **Analyze song (tempo, key and sections)…**: reads the click, the key and
  the voice guide and suggests the tempo, the key and the markers. See
  [Analyze song](/docs/song-analysis/).
- **Delete Song** (<kbd>Del</kbd>).

### On a marker

![A marker's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-marker.png)

- **Jump to This Marker**: moves the cursor to the marker.
- **Rename** (<kbd>F2</kbd>).
- **Exact position...**: type the exact position, as *bar.beat* or in
  seconds.
- **Marker type...**: Intro, Verse, Chorus… or a cue like *Drums In*. The type
  sets the colour and what the voice guide says.
- **Delete**.

### On a tempo marker

![A tempo marker's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-tempo-marker.png)

Tempo markers (the flag with the BPM, in the lower part of the ruler) have
their own menu:

- **Change BPM**: the tempo from that marker on.
- **Ramp to the next marker**: instead of jumping at once, the tempo rises or
  falls gradually to the next marker's and reaches it right there. The marker
  is drawn sloped in the ruler, and the metronome and the voice guide follow
  the ramp. It needs another BPM marker after it. See
  [Tempo and time signature](/docs/tasks/tempo/#ramping-the-tempo-up-or-down).
- **Delete Marker**.

### Several markers at once

![Three markers selected in the ruler](/guide/desktop-en/ruler-marker-selection.png)

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click on a marker adds it to or removes it from the selection
without scheduling the jump, and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click selects every marker
between the last one clicked and this one (sections, cues, tempo and time
signature). With several selected:

- Drag any of them to move the whole group.
- <kbd>Del</kbd> deletes them and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> duplicates them
  after the group, in whole bars.
- Right-click opens the group's menu.

On mobile, with **Select by area** on, tap the markers or drag your finger
along the ruler to add them.

Markers can also be dragged along the ruler to move them.

### On a tempo change

![A tempo change's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-tempo.png)

- **Change BPM** of that section.
- **Delete Marker**: the section goes back to the previous tempo.

### On the empty ruler

![The ruler's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-ruler.png)

- **Create marker** at the point you clicked. You pick its type first.
- **Change Timeline BPM**: the session's starting tempo.
- **Create time-signature marker**: a change of time signature (to 6/8, for
  example) from that point.
- **Create automation**: an automatic action at that point. See
  [Automation](/docs/automation/).
- **Clear Timeline Selection**.

If you first drag on the ruler to select a stretch, the first option becomes
**Create Song From Selection**.

### On a track

![A track's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-track.png)

- **Insert Track** and **Insert Folder Track**, right below.
- **Reset track height**.
- **Rename** (<kbd>F2</kbd>) and **Select colour…**.
- **Channels…**: stereo, mono (sum), left only or right only, and **Split
  L/R channels** for a file with the click on one side.
- **Delete**.
- **Indent Into Previous Folder** and **Remove From Folder**.
- **Add MIDI track**: to send notes or program changes to keyboards, lights
  or external software.

A folder's menu is the same.

### On a clip

![A clip's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-clip.png)

- **Split At Cursor** (<kbd>S</kbd>): splits the clip at the cursor position.
- **Duplicate** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd>).
- **Select colour…**.
- **Delete** (<kbd>Del</kbd>).

Splitting, moving or duplicating clips **never changes your audio files**: it
only changes which part of the audio plays and where.

### On the automation track

![The automation track's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-automation.png)

- **Create automation here**.
- **Manage mix scenes...**: saves whole mixes to apply them in one go.
- **Remove automation track**: also deletes all of its automation.

See [Automation](/docs/automation/).
