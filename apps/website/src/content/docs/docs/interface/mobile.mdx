On phone and tablet, LibreTracks is the same app as on the computer: the same
engine, the same session format and the same views. The app is always used
**in landscape**. What changes is how you touch it:

- **There is no right-click**: **press and hold** to open something's menu.
- **There is no File menu**: its options are in the side bar.
- The actions for whatever you have selected show up in a **floating bar** at
  the bottom.

This page goes through those differences. Gestures, where sessions are kept
and the audio output are in [LibreTracks on mobile](/docs/mobile/).

## Start screen

![The start screen on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/landing.png)

<Legend>

1. **Sessions**: the list of your sessions, to open another one without
   leaving the one you have open.
2. **Save** the open session.
3. **File**: import a song, manage the cloud, export the session and save it
   as a template. See [below](#the-file-button).
4. **Library**: the session's audio.
5. **Settings**.
6. **Tutorial**: the guided tours, adapted to the touch screen.
7. **Create** a new session.
8. **Import session**: opens an `.ltset`, from the phone or from your Google
   Drive.
9. **Cloud**: connect or disconnect your Google Drive account, and delete what
   you have uploaded.
10. **Demo song**: an example session with two songs, to try things out
    without importing anything.

</Legend>

The **Remote** is not in this bar: it is for controlling the computer *from*
the phone, with the browser. See [Custom Remote](/docs/remote-control/).

### The File button

![The File button's options](/guide/mobile-en/file-actions.png)

- **Import song**: adds an `.ltpkg` song to the open session.
- **Cloud**: manages your Google Drive account (connect, disconnect, delete
  what you uploaded).
- **Export session…**: the whole session, with its audio, in an `.ltset`.
- **Save as template…**.

The last two are only enabled with a session open. As on the computer, with
the cloud connected **Import** and **Export** ask whether to use the phone or
your Google Drive.

## The work screen

![The DAW view on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/daw-overview.png)

<Legend>

1. **BPM** of the song under the cursor.
2. **Tap**: tap along to work out the tempo.
3. **Time signature**.
4. **Undo** and **redo**.
5. **Transport**, metronome, voice guide and pads, as on the computer. See
   [the top bar](/docs/interface/main-screen/#transport).
6. **Position** in bars and in time.
7. **CPU**: the audio engine's load. Tap it for the details.
8. **Track buttons**. See [below](#track-buttons).
9. **Action bar**: with nothing selected, it is for **adding**. See
   [below](#adding).

</Legend>

Below the top bar is the same [toolbar](/docs/interface/toolbar/) as on the
computer; swipe across it to see the groups that don't fit.

### Track buttons

![The buttons above the track list](/guide/mobile-en/track-header-actions.png)

<Legend>

1. **Reorder tracks**: turns on a mode where you drag tracks to change their
   order. Tap it again to leave.
2. **Shorter tracks**.
3. **Taller tracks**.
4. **Lock the ruler**: with the padlock on, tapping the ruler does not move
   the cursor. It avoids accidental jumps while you play live.

</Legend>

### Adding

With nothing selected, the action bar offers **Section** (a section marker at
the cursor), **Cue** (a cue marker) and **Audio** (import audio into the
session). The **…** button shows every option:

![Every Add option](/guide/mobile-en/add-sheet.png)

Besides section, cue and audio, there are **Video from the gallery** and
**Video from files**, to add a video that is projected with the song (see
[Video on the projector](/docs/tasks/video/)).

The **⌄** arrow folds the bar away to see more of the timeline.

### Resource meter

![The resource meter's details](/guide/mobile-en/resource-panel.png)

On mobile the **CPU** pill shows the **audio load**: the share of each block
of sound's time that the engine needs to process it. Above 100 % you hear
dropouts. **Audio dropouts** counts the ones there have been so far.

## Selecting a track

Tap a track's **name** to select it. The action bar changes and shows what you
can do with it:

![A selected track and its action bar](/guide/mobile-en/track-selected.png)

<Legend>

1. The **selected track**, highlighted.
2. What is selected.
3. **Quick actions**: insert track, insert folder, reset the height…
4. **More actions**: the track's full menu.
5. **Mix**: shows the mix controls next to the bar. See below.
6. **Select several**: from here on, every track you tap is added to the
   selection.
7. **Clear selection**.
8. **Hide** the bar.

</Legend>

On a phone the track headers only show **M** and **S**. The rest of the mix
appears when you select the track: a row above it with its **T**, volume, pan
and output. The **Mix** button brings those controls next to the action bar:

![The selected track's mix controls](/guide/mobile-en/track-mix.png)

## Selecting a clip

Tap a clip to select it:

![A clip's action bar](/guide/mobile-en/clip-selected.png)

The clip's actions, the same as its
[computer menu](/docs/interface/timeline/#on-a-clip), show up in the bar:
**Split At Cursor** (enabled when the cursor is over the clip),
**Duplicate**, **Select colour…**, and the ones that don't fit under **…**.

With the clip selected, drag it to move it. That way an accidental brush never
moves the audio.

## Press and hold

Press and hold a song, a marker or any point on the timeline to open its menu,
the same as a right-click on the computer.

![A song's menu after pressing and holding it](/guide/mobile-en/song-sheet.png)

![A marker's menu after pressing and holding it](/guide/mobile-en/marker-sheet.png)

What each option does is in
[Right-click menus](/docs/interface/timeline/#right-click-menus).

## Library

![The Library on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/library.png)

<Legend>

1. **Add folder**: pick a folder on the phone or the SD card with Android's
   picker. It stays saved.
2. **Search** what you have expanded.
3. **A folder you added**. Tap it to expand it.
4. **Add as a song**: the button on each folder brings it whole to the cursor
   as a song, with one track per audio file.
5. **A selected audio file**: tap audio files to select them.
6. **Add to timeline**: puts the selected files on new tracks at the cursor
   position. The **X** clears the selection.
7. **In this session**: the audio already in the session.

</Legend>

**In this session** works like an accordion: when you expand it, the session's
library (**Folder**, **Import audio** and the audio sorted into folders) takes
the whole panel; collapse it and your folders come back.

![“In this session” expanded on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/library-session.png)

Tap a file to select it and its actions appear. To close the Library, tap its
button in the side bar again. More in
[Your folders in the Library](/docs/mobile/#your-folders-in-the-library). As on the computer, the audio
is used **without copying it** into the session (this can be changed): see
[Importing audio without copying it](/docs/mobile/#importing-audio-without-copying-it).

## Settings

![Settings on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/settings.png)

The same tabs as on the computer ([see Settings](/docs/interface/settings/)),
except **Shortcuts**, which makes no sense without a keyboard. On mobile there
are also options of its own: where to keep sessions (internal storage, SD card
or USB stick), importing without copying and the low-latency output. They are
explained in [LibreTracks on mobile](/docs/mobile/).

## Compact View and Live View

The three views are on the first three buttons of the toolbar, as on the
computer.

![Compact View on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/compact.png)

**Compact View** shows one column per song with its tracks, the button to play
it and its volume fader. **+ New song** and **Import .ltpkg** are at the end,
and **Mixer** opens the mixer at the bottom. See
[Compact View](/docs/compact-view/).

![Live View on mobile](/guide/mobile-en/live.png)

**Live View** is the one for playing: the song's markers as big buttons, with
how long until the next one, the setlist at the bottom and the jump settings
always at hand. See [Live View](/docs/live-view/).

## Sessions

![The session list](/guide/mobile-en/sessions.png)

The **Sessions** button in the side bar opens this window without closing the
current session. From here you create, import or open another one. To delete a
session from the device, use the bin next to its name; the one you have open
cannot be deleted.
