The toolbar sits right below the top bar. On the left you switch views and
editing modes. On the right you decide **how** the show moves: what happens
when you press Vamp, when a jump happens and how each song sounds.

Most groups have a <kbd>☰</kbd> button (three lines with sliders) that opens
their settings panel. The summary shown in the group, like *At next marker*,
is the setting that is active right now.

## View and editing

![The left part of the toolbar](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-left.png)

<Legend>

1. **DAW view**: the full timeline, for building and editing songs.
2. **Compact View**: one column per song, with a shared mixer. See
   [Compact View](/docs/compact-view/).
3. **Live View**: big buttons for playing live, with no editing. See
   [Live View](/docs/live-view/).
4. **Snap** (to the grid): when you move clips, markers or songs, they stick to
   the grid's subdivision. Turn it off to place them freely. With snap off, a
   diagonal line over the icon shows it.
5. **Follow the cursor**: during playback the timeline scrolls by itself so
   the cursor does not leave the screen.
6. **Vamp**: repeats a stretch in a loop until you press it again. See
   [below](#vamp).
7. **Marker Jump**: when a jump to a marker happens. See
   [below](#marker-jump).

</Legend>

## Jumps and song settings

![The right part of the toolbar](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-right.png)

<Legend>

8. **Song Transition**: when and how you move to another song. See
   [below](#song-transition).
9. **Master** of the selected song.
10. **Transpose** of the selected song, in semitones.
11. **Warp** of the selected song.
12. **Cancel jump**: cancels a jump that is scheduled and has not happened
    yet. Also with <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

</Legend>

Groups 9, 10 and 11 act on **the selected song**. Click a song's name on the
ruler to select it. With no song selected, the group reminds you to select a
region.

## Vamp

![The Vamp settings panel](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-vamp-panel.png)

The group's **Vamp** button turns the loop on and off. In its panel you choose
what repeats:

- **Section**: the section the cursor is in, from its marker to the next one.
- **Bars**: the number of bars you set, starting at the beginning of the bar
  you are in.

Handy when the band stretches an ending, the pastor keeps talking or someone
needs more time on stage. Press Vamp again to leave it and the song carries on
from there.

## Marker Jump

![The marker jump settings panel](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-marker-jump-panel.png)

A jump to a marker is scheduled when you click a section marker, press its
number (<kbd>1</kbd>…<kbd>9</kbd>, <kbd>0</kbd>), from the Remote or with a MIDI
pedal. This panel decides **when** it happens:

- **Immediate**: right away.
- **After X bars**: once the bars you set have gone by.
- **At next marker**: when the next section marker arrives, so the change
  lands at the start of a phrase.

While the jump waits, *Cancel jump* lights up; hover over it to see which
marker you are going to. If you picked the wrong marker, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or
click the same marker again.

## Song Transition

![The song transition panel](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-song-jump-panel.png)

It applies when you jump to another song: with the *Previous* and *Next*
buttons, with <kbd>Shift</kbd>+number, from the Live View or from the Remote. The
top row says **when**:

- **Immediate**.
- **At song end** of the one that is playing.
- **After X bars**.
- **At next marker**.

The bottom row says **how**: **Clean cut** goes straight to the other song;
**Fade out** lowers the current one before jumping.

## Master, transposition and warp

![Master of the selected song](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-master-panel.png)

**Master** raises or lowers the volume of the whole selected song, without
touching the metronome or the voice guide. It is for evening out songs that
come in louder or quieter.

![Transposition of the selected song](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-transpose-panel.png)

**Transpose** changes the key of the selected song between −12 and +12
semitones with the <kbd>−</kbd> and <kbd>+</kbd> buttons.

![Warp of the selected song](/guide/desktop-en/toolbar-warp-panel.png)

**Warp** changes the tempo without changing the pitch. With warp off,
transposing changes the speed too, like a sped-up record; with warp on, the
song keeps its length. The audio's original BPM is set from the song's menu,
with *Change Original BPM*.

How transposition, warp and each track's **T** button combine is explained
step by step in [Pitch, warp and the T button](/docs/pitch-and-warp/).
