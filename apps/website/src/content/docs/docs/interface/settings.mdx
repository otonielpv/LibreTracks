Open Settings with the **Settings** button in the side bar. Changes are saved
on their own straight away, except the hardware outputs, which have their own
*Apply* button.

![The Settings window and its tabs](/guide/desktop-en/settings-overview.png)

<Legend>

1. [**General**](#general): opens into Interface, Timeline, Playback,
   Editing, Storage and System.
2. [**Audio**](#audio): sound card, outputs and performance.
3. [**MIDI**](#midi): opens into Devices and MIDI Learn.
4. [**Video**](#video): the screen or projector for video tracks.
5. [**Redundancy**](/docs/redundancy/): a second, synchronised backup
   computer.
6. [**Shortcuts**](#shortcuts): the keys for each action.
7. [**Diagnostics**](#diagnostics): logs for when something goes wrong.
8. **Close**. Also with <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

General and MIDI are groups: clicking one opens it and shows its first tab.
Both can be open at the same time.

</Legend>

## General

The app's settings, split across six tabs.

### Interface

![The Interface tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-general.png)

- The app's **Language**.
- **Interface size**: make it smaller if it doesn't fit your screen, or bigger
  to read it from a distance. Shortcuts: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd>,
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>−</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd>. It goes up to 150%.
- **Mixer size**: makes only the mixer's strips, faders and M/S/T buttons
  bigger, leaving the rest of the interface as it is. *Automatic* makes them
  large on tablets and normal elsewhere. Each device keeps its own.
- **Startup view**: the view sessions open in.

### Timeline

![The Timeline tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-timeline.png)

- **Timeline navigation**: how the mouse wheel and the trackpad respond. In
  *Ableton* mode, pinching or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel zooms;
  <kbd>Alt</kbd>+wheel changes the height of the track under the mouse.
- **Playhead follow**: where the cursor stays when the timeline follows it.

### Playback

![The Playback tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-playback.png)

- **Pause at the end of each song**: instead of moving on to the next one by
  itself, playback stops at the end of each song. Ideal when someone has to
  speak between songs.
- **Vamp between songs (click and pad)**: like the pause, but between songs
  the click keeps going at the tempo of the one that ended and the pad keeps
  sounding until you press *Play*. See
  [Going from one song to the next](/docs/tasks/songs/#going-from-one-song-to-the-next-live).
- **"Fade out and stop" length**: how many seconds that button takes to fade
  the song out (5 by default). See
  [Fade out and stop](/docs/interface/main-screen/#fade-out-and-stop).

### Editing

![The Editing tab: songs and templates](/guide/desktop-en/settings-editing-1.png)

- **Each song's tracks**: whether songs share tracks, each gets its own
  folder, or its own copy of the tracks. See
  [Song templates](/docs/song-templates/#each-songs-tracks).
- **Template for new songs**: which tracks and folders new songs come with
  and where the audio you drop lands. See [Song templates](/docs/song-templates/).
- **My templates**: the templates on this computer, to import them or upload
  them to the cloud.

![The Editing tab: colour, warp and saving](/guide/desktop-en/settings-editing-2.png)

- **Colour new tracks automatically**.
- **Automatic warp**: turns warp on when you change the pitch or the tempo.
  See [Automatic warp](/docs/pitch-and-warp/#automatic-warp).
- **Autosave**: saves the session every so often, never while you play.
  **Save every**: how many minutes.

### Storage

![The Storage tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-storage.png)

- **Use the classic library**: the Library shows only the audio imported into
  the session, with its virtual folders, as before, instead of the folders on
  your disk. See [The Library](/docs/interface/side-nav-library/#the-library).
- **Audio cache**: MP3, FLAC and other compressed audio is decompressed once
  and kept here, so the session loads instantly next time. You can change the
  folder, give it a maximum size or empty it to free up space.

### System

![The System tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-system.png)

- **Check for updates**: checks whether there is a new version. The app also
  tells you by itself when it starts.
- **Optional usage statistics**: aggregated, anonymous data that helps improve
  LibreTracks. Audio, names and the content of your sessions are never sent.

## Audio

![The Audio tab, first part](/guide/desktop-en/settings-audio-1.png)

- **Audio System**: the kind of driver. The list depends on your computer. On
  Windows, **ASIO** has the lowest delay if your interface supports it, and
  **Windows Audio** (WASAPI) works with any sound card. Avoid **DirectSound**
  for playing live.
- **Audio device**: the sound card or interface the sound goes out of.
- **Refresh audio devices**: looks for interfaces again, for example if you
  plugged one in with the app open.
- **Sample rate**: leave it on *Auto* unless your interface needs a specific
  one. When you open a session, LibreTracks tries to match the audio's rate so
  it doesn't have to convert it.

![The Audio tab, second part](/guide/desktop-en/settings-audio-2.png)

- **Buffer size**: smaller means less delay; bigger means less risk of
  dropouts. If you hear clicks or dropouts, raise it.
- **Hardware outputs**: tick the channels of your interface you want to use
  and press **Apply**. Only the ticked channels show up as outputs on the
  tracks. See [Audio outputs and metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/).
- **Multicore processing**: spreads the audio work across several processor
  cores. Leave it on.
- **Track meters**: what each track's meter measures. By default,
  **Post-fader** (after pan, volume, mute and solo, like Ableton): a muted
  track reads zero. **Post-pan** measures before the volume and
  **Pre-fader**, what comes into the track.

## MIDI

The MIDI group has two tabs.

### Devices

![The Devices tab: input and outputs](/guide/desktop-en/settings-midi-1.png)

- **MIDI input device**: the pedal, pad or controller you want to drive
  LibreTracks with. The button on the right looks for devices again.
- **MIDI output**: the port MIDI tracks send notes and program changes
  through, to keyboards, lighting desks or lyrics software. **Send test note**
  checks that it gets there.
- **Controller output (LEDs)**: the port your fader controller listens on for
  mute, solo, play and vamp state. Usually named like its input.

![The Devices tab: strips and takeover](/guide/desktop-en/settings-midi-2.png)

- **Strips follow the active song**: the controller's 8 faders move only the
  tracks of the playing song and go back to the first bank when the song
  changes.
- **Takeover mode**: what happens when the physical fader isn't where the
  track is. See [Fader controllers](/docs/tasks/midi/#strips-1-8-profiles-and-motorized-faders).

### MIDI Learn

![The MIDI Learn tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-midiLearn-1.png)

There are two ways to assign a pedal or a button on your controller to an
action:

- **From the list**: press **Relearn** on the action's row and then step on
  the pedal.
- **From the screen**: press **MIDI Learn**, close Settings, click the button
  you want to control (*Play*, *Vamp*, a marker, the BPM…) and step on the
  pedal. Press *MIDI Learn* again to leave.

The assignment shows in the **Current MIDI binding** column, and the last one
you made, at the top. **Reset MIDI mappings** clears them all. The **Markers**
and **Songs** tabs assign jumps to specific markers and songs.

## Video

![The Video tab: output and display](/guide/desktop-en/settings-video-1.png)

- **Video output**: turn it on so video tracks go out to another screen.
- **Display**: the projector or the TV. **Identify displays** shows a number
  on each one so you can tell which is which.
- **Mode**: fullscreen or window.

![The Video tab: behaviour](/guide/desktop-en/settings-video-2.png)

- **Fit**: how the picture adapts to the screen.
- **Idle screen**: what shows when no video is playing.
- **When stopped**: what stays on screen when you stop playback.

![The Video tab: sync](/guide/desktop-en/settings-video-3.png)

- **Delay compensation**: if the projector shows the picture late compared
  with the audio, bring it forward here. **Calibrate…** and **Test pattern**
  help you measure it.
- **When placing a video with sound**: ask, always extract its audio to a
  track, or never extract it. See
  [Video on the projector](/docs/tasks/video/#the-sound-of-the-video).

## Shortcuts

![The Shortcuts tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-shortcuts-1.png)

Search for an action, press **Edit** and then the key combination. The **×**
removes the shortcut and **Reset all** goes back to the factory ones. If
another action already uses the combination, the app tells you. The full list
of default shortcuts is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/live-control-flow/#keyboard-shortcuts).

## Diagnostics

![The Diagnostics tab](/guide/desktop-en/settings-diagnostics.png)

If the app closes, freezes or a track goes silent, here are the logs that help
us find the cause. **Save log...** creates a file you can send when you ask
for help. The logs contain no audio and no personal data.
