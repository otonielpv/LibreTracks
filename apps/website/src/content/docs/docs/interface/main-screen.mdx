When you open a session, LibreTracks shows the **DAW view**: the timeline with
your songs from left to right and the tracks from top to bottom. This page
goes through the screen area by area and explains the top bar button by
button. Click any screenshot to see it full size.

![The LibreTracks main screen with its ten areas numbered](/guide/desktop-en/daw-zones.png)

<Legend>

1. **File menu**: create, open, save, import and export sessions.
   [See the full menu](#file-menu).
2. **Tempo and time signature** of the song under the cursor, the *Tap*
   button and undo/redo. [Below](#tempo-time-signature-and-undo).
3. **Transport**: play, pause and stop, and the metronome, voice guide and
   pads switches. [Below](#transport).
4. **Readouts**: tempo, time signature, position and elapsed time.
   [Below](#readouts-and-resource-meter).
5. **Resource meter**: CPU, memory, disk and audio load.
6. **Side bar**: Library, Remote, Settings and Tutorial.
7. **Toolbar**: view switcher, snap, follow the cursor, Vamp, jumps and the
   settings of the selected song (master, pitch and warp).
8. **Ruler**: the session's songs, the bars, the section markers and the
   tempo changes. Click it to move the cursor.
9. **Track headers**: mute (M), solo (S), transposition (T), volume, pan and
   audio output of each track.
10. **Tracks**: the audio clips, the MIDI tracks and the automation track, on
    the timeline.

</Legend>

## Tempo, time signature and undo

![The left part of the top bar](/guide/desktop-en/topbar-left.png)

<Legend>

1. **File**: opens the [File menu](#file-menu).
2. **BPM**: the tempo. Type a number and press Enter. It changes the tempo
   section the cursor is in, and the label on its right tells you which one:
   *Tempo @ 00:00.000* means "the tempo that starts at second 0".
3. **Tap**: tap along with the music four or five times and LibreTracks works
   out the tempo and applies it to that same section.
4. **Time signature** (4/4, 6/8, 3/4…). With the cursor at the start of the
   session it changes the starting time signature; anywhere else, it creates a
   time-signature change there. See [Tempo and time signature](/docs/tasks/tempo/).
5. **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) the last change to the session.
6. **Redo** (Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y) what you just undid.

</Legend>

:::tip[Each song with its own tempo]
Tempo and time signature are not global: each song in the session has its
own. Put the cursor on the song you want to change before typing the BPM or
using *Tap*.
:::

## Transport

![The transport, metronome, voice guide and pads buttons](/guide/desktop-en/topbar-transport.png)

<Legend>

1. **Previous**: jumps to the song before the one that is playing. Like
   *Next*, it follows the *Song Jump* setting. From the first song it goes to
   the last.
2. **Stop** (Shift+Space): stops playback and takes the cursor back to the
   start of the session.
3. **Fade out and stop**: fades out the song that is playing, then stops.
   See [below](#fade-out-and-stop).
4. **Play** (Space) from the cursor position.
5. **Pause** (Space): stops playback and leaves the cursor where it is, to
   carry on from there.
6. **Click**: turns the metronome on or off.
7. **Metronome settings**: output, volume, sounds, accent and subdivision.
   See [Audio outputs and metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/).
8. **Guide**: turns the voice guide on or off; it announces each section
   before it arrives.
9. **Voice guide settings**. See [Voice guide](/docs/voice-guide/).
10. **Pads**: turns the ambient pad on or off.
11. **Pads settings**: key, volume, fades and your own pads. See
    [Ambient pads](/docs/ambient-pads/).
12. **Next**: jumps to the next song in the session. When the jump happens
    (immediately, at the end of the song, after a few bars…) is set by the
    *Song Jump* setting in the toolbar. From the last song it goes back to the
    first.

</Legend>

The little arrows next to *Click*, *Guide* and *Pads* open their settings
panel without going to Settings:

### Fade out and stop

To end a song live without a hard cut. While it plays, press **Fade out and
stop**: the song fades out and, when the fade ends, the transport stops. The
button stays **amber** while the fade lasts.

- **Pressing it again** during the fade stops at once: it is the emergency
  exit.
- Only the **tracks** fade. The click and the voice guide stay at their level
  until it stops.
- Pressing **Stop** or **Pause** during the fade cancels it; when you play
  again, the song is back at its normal level.
- The length is set in
  [Settings › General](/docs/interface/settings/#general) (5 seconds by
  default).
- It can also be triggered by a **keyboard shortcut** (unassigned out of the
  box: give it one in Settings › Shortcuts), a **MIDI pedal** (MIDI learn) and
  the [Remote](/docs/remote-control/).

## Readouts and resource meter

![The resource meter and position readouts](/guide/desktop-en/topbar-right.png)

<Legend>

1. **Resource meter**: CPU, memory (RAM), disk reads and **audio load**.
   Audio load is the share of each block of sound's time that the engine needs
   to process it: above 100 % you hear dropouts. Click it for the details.
2. **Tempo** playing at the current position.
3. **Time signature** playing at the current position.
4. **Bar**: the musical position, as *bar.beat.fraction*. "12.3.00" is the
   start of the third beat of bar 12.
5. **Timecode**: the time elapsed since the start of the session.
6. Transport **status**: stopped, playing or paused.

</Legend>

## File menu

![The File menu, open](/guide/desktop-en/file-menu.png)

- **New project**: creates an empty session in a new folder.
- **New from template…**: creates a session from an `.lttemplate`, with its
  tracks and outputs already set up.
- **Open** and **Open recent**: open an `.ltsession`.
- **Import song**: adds an exported song (`.ltpkg`) to the open session, with
  its audio, markers and settings.
- **Import session…** and **Export session…**: move a whole session, with all
  its audio, in a single `.ltset` file. It is how you take it to another
  computer or to your phone.
- **Cloud…**: connects or disconnects your Google Drive account, shows the
  space used and lets you delete the songs and sessions you uploaded.
- **Import Reaper/Ableton**: builds the structure from an `.rpp` or `.als`
  project.
- **Save** (Ctrl+S) and **Save as** (Ctrl+Shift+S).
- **Save as template…**: saves the tracks and outputs, without audio, to reuse
  them in your next session.
- **Exit**: saves the session, tells you where it was saved and closes the
  app.

With the cloud connected, **Import** and **Export** (of songs and sessions)
first ask where: **This device** or your **Google Drive**. In other words,
uploading and downloading happen from those same buttons; the Cloud one is
only for managing the account.

More on these formats in
[Export, import and share](/docs/integration-ecosystem/).
