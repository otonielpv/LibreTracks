The side bar is the narrow column on the left. It is always visible, with or
without a session open.

![The side bar buttons](/guide/desktop-en/side-nav.png)

<Legend>

1. **Library**: opens or closes the panel with the session's audio. See
   [below](#the-library).
2. **Remote**: shows the QR code and the address to control LibreTracks from a
   phone or tablet. See [below](#remote).
3. **Settings**: audio, interface, video, shortcuts, MIDI and diagnostics. See
   [Settings](/docs/interface/settings/).
4. **Tutorial**: guided tours inside the app itself.

</Legend>

## The Library

The Library keeps all the audio you have brought into the session, whether it
is used on the timeline or not. From here you import it, sort it into folders
and drag it onto the tracks.

![The Library panel](/guide/desktop-en/library-panel.png)

<Legend>

1. **Folder**: creates a folder to sort the audio. They are *virtual*
   folders: they do not move or copy anything on disk.
2. **Import audio**: pick WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC… files. You can also drag them
   from your file manager straight onto the timeline: they are imported and
   placed where you drop them.
3. **Unfiled**: the audio that is not in any folder yet.
4. **An audio file**, with its length. Drag it onto the timeline to create a
   clip.

</Legend>

You can also drag **a whole folder** onto the timeline: each audio file goes to
its own track, one below the other, like a song's stems. If you hold
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> when you drop, they go one after the other on the same track.

To select several files use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click, and
drag them together. <kbd>Del</kbd> removes the selection from the Library.
Importing does not copy the audio (the session uses it from where it is), and
removing it from the Library **does not delete your original file**. More in
[Library and audio](/docs/tasks/library/).

### Right-click on a file

![A Library file's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-library-asset.png)

- **Move to folder…** and **Move to root**.
- **Delete** the file from the Library.

### Right-click on a folder

![A Library folder's menu](/guide/desktop-en/menu-library-folder.png)

- **Add to timeline as a song**: creates a new song with all the audio in the
  folder, each file on its own track. It is the fastest way to build a song
  from its stems: one folder per song, and one click.
- **Create virtual folder**.

Folders you created also show **Rename folder** and **Delete folder**.

## Remote

![The Remote connection window](/guide/desktop-en/remote-modal.png)

The computer and the phone have to be on the **same Wi‑Fi network**. Scan the
QR code with the phone's camera, or type one of the two addresses into its
browser. Nothing needs to be installed on the phone. What you can do from
there, and how to customise its screens, is in
[Custom Remote](/docs/remote-control/).

## Tutorial

![The guided tours menu](/guide/desktop-en/tutorial-menu.png)

Each tour points at the real controls on the screen, step by step. Some steps
ask you to open the panel they talk about and carry on by themselves when you
do. The ones you have done are ticked, and you can replay them whenever you
like.
