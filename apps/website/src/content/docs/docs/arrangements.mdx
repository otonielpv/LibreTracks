An **arrangement** changes the shape of a song without setting up jumps: two
more choruses at the end, drop the bridge, repeat the verse. LibreTracks
**writes it onto the timeline as it is**: if you repeat the verse, you see the
verse twice, and the songs after it move along on their own. That is why the
voice guide, the Remote, rendering and exported songs follow it with nothing
else to do.

You can keep **several arrangements** of the same song (Sunday's, the short
one, the acoustic one) and go back to the original whenever you like.

## Before you start: the sections

An arrangement is built from the song's **sections**: Intro, Verse, Chorus…
Mark them in the sections row of the ruler, as usual (see
[Markers](/docs/tasks/markers/)). You need at least two. Cue markers (Build,
Drums in…) do not split sections: they travel with the section they sit in.

## Saving the original

Right-click the song on the ruler and choose **Arrangement** (on mobile,
long-press the song). In the Compact view it is in the song's menu. The
arrangement editor opens at the bottom, over the timeline.

![First step of the arrangement editor: the detected sections and Save as original](/guide/desktop-en/arrangement-capture.png)

<Legend>

1. **Detected sections**: the ones LibreTracks found in the song, with their
   color and length in bars.
2. **Save as original**: saves the song as it is, so you can build any
   arrangement and come back to it whenever you like.

</Legend>

If a marker does not fall on the first beat of a bar, the editor warns you
(repeating that section would put it out of step) and offers **Snap to bar**.

![Arrangement editor with its zones numbered](/guide/desktop-en/arrangement-editor.png)

<Legend>

1. **Saved arrangement**: the picker with your arrangements. The first one is
   always **Original**.
2. **New**, **Rename**, **Delete** and **Save the original again**. *New*
   starts an arrangement from the original.
3. **Sections**: the palette with the song's sections. **Click** one to add it
   at the end; **drag** it onto the strip below to insert it where you drop it.
4. **Order**: the arrangement's blocks, in the order they will play. Drag them
   to move them around. With a block selected, <kbd>Del</kbd> removes it and
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> duplicates it. The total length in bars is
   shown underneath.
5. **Apply**: writes the arrangement onto the timeline. Until you press it,
   the changes live only in the editor (*Unapplied changes* says so).

</Legend>

When it is applied, the following songs line up behind it, on their bar. With
an arrangement applied, the song carries its name on the ruler (`⇄ Sunday`),
and the ruler slightly shades the sections that are repeats.

![A song with an arrangement applied on the ruler](/guide/desktop-en/arrangement-ruler.png)

- To **go back to the original**, choose *Original* in the picker and press
  **Apply**. Your arrangements are not deleted.
- The original cannot be deleted or renamed. If you change its blocks, a new
  arrangement is created from it.
- If you saved the original by mistake (a marker was missing, a section was
  in the wrong place), fix the song on the timeline and use **Save the
  original again**.

## Editing a song with an arrangement

Track volume, mute, pan and outputs change as usual.

**Each block can be edited on its own.** If you repeat the chorus, the copy
brings everything the original chorus has: clips, MIDI, automation, cues and
video settings. But you can move, trim or add clips, create an automation,
send a special MIDI message or change the video in the second chorus only:
the change stays in that block and leaves the first one and the original
alone. In the arrangement editor that block shows as **edited**, and **Reset
to the original** makes it a copy of the original chorus again.

What shapes the song is changed in the original: **section markers**,
**tempo**, **meter**, **key** and the song's length. For those, LibreTracks
asks first: *This song has an arrangement*. **Edit original** takes the song
back to the original (the arrangement is kept), you edit whatever you need
and, when you press **Apply** again, the arrangement is rebuilt on the edited
song:

- Blocks that follow the original get the changes.
- Edited blocks keep their own content and do not get them.
- If you removed a section that an arrangement used, it tells you which
  arrangements it was removed from and whether an edited block was lost
  with it.
- If a section got shorter, whatever its edited blocks had past the new end
  is trimmed, and it tells you.

## Where the arrangement shows

- In the **Compact view**, the song header shows the applied arrangement;
  press it to open the editor.
- In the [Live View](/docs/live-view/) and on the [Remote](/docs/remote-control/),
  the song in the setlist carries its arrangement's name.
- Arrangements travel in exported songs (`.ltpkg`) and sessions (`.ltset`),
  and [rendering](/docs/tasks/songs/#exporting-rendering-and-deleting) outputs
  the song as it sounds with its arrangement.

If you open the session with an older version of LibreTracks, you will see the
arrangement written on the timeline and it will sound the same, but saving
from that version loses the arrangement's original, and applying the
arrangement again there loses the changes of edited blocks.

## On phones and tablets

Long-press the song and choose **Arrangement**: the editor takes the whole
screen. The first time, press **Save as original**, as on the computer.

![Arrangement editor on the phone](/guide/mobile-en/arrangement-editor.png)

<Legend>

1. **Saved arrangement**: the arrangement picker and the one that is applied.
2. **The arrangement's blocks**, in the order they will play. Drag by the
   handle to reorder, swipe a block left to remove it and long-press to
   duplicate it. After removing a section you have a few seconds to **Undo**.
3. **+**: opens the palette to insert sections at that point. At the end of
   the list, **Add section** adds them after everything. In the palette, tap a
   section to add it (as many times as you like) and press **Done**.
4. **More options**: New, Rename, Delete and Save the original again.

</Legend>

![The phone's section palette](/guide/mobile-en/arrangement-add-sheet.png)

When the arrangement is the way you want it, press **Apply**.
