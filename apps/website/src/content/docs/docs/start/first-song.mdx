This walkthrough takes you from a freshly installed app to a song ready to
play live. You need the song's **stems** (one audio file per instrument:
drums, bass, keys, click, guide…) in a folder on your computer. Each step links
to its page with all the details.

If you would rather watch it before reading it, this video does the whole
walkthrough in the app, with narration and LibreTracks' own sound:

Would you rather play around first? On mobile, the **Demo song** on the start
screen opens an example session without importing anything. And at any time,
the **Tutorial** button in the side bar shows you the controls on the app
itself.

## 1. Create the session

When you open LibreTracks you see the start screen:

![The start screen](/guide/desktop-en/landing-overview.png)

<Legend>

1. **Create**: a new session. You choose a name and a folder; LibreTracks
   creates the session's folder there.
2. **Open**: a session you already have (`.ltsession`).
3. **Import session**: a whole exported session (`.ltset`), for example from
   another computer. If the cloud is connected, it asks whether to take it
   from this computer or from your Google Drive.
4. **Import Reaper/Ableton**: start from an `.rpp` or `.als` project.
5. **Cloud**: connect or disconnect your Google Drive account, and see or
   delete what you have uploaded. To bring something down from the cloud you
   use **Import**.
6. **Templates**: start with the tracks and outputs of an earlier session.
   Your **recent** sessions appear on the right.

</Legend>

Press **Create**. A **session** is your setlist: it can hold one song or the
whole Sunday set.

## 2. Bring in the stems

1. Open the **Library** with its button in the side bar.
2. **Import audio** and choose all the song's stems.
3. Create a **folder** in the Library with the song's name and put the stems
   in it.
4. Drag the **folder's name** onto the timeline.

LibreTracks creates the song with the folder's name and one audio file per
track, all starting together. See [Library and audio](/docs/tasks/library/).

LibreTracks uses the files from where they are: don't move or delete them
while you use the session.

## 3. Set the tempo

In the top bar, type the song's **BPM** (or use **Tap**) and, if it isn't 4/4,
the **time signature**. Turn on **Click** and press **Play**: the click has to
land with the drums. See [Tempo and time signature](/docs/tasks/tempo/).

## 4. Mark the sections

For each part of the song: right-click the ruler where it starts › **Create
marker** › **Sections** › Intro, Verse, Chorus… Add **Cues** like *Drums In*
or *Last Time* wherever you want the band to hear a direction. See
[Markers](/docs/tasks/markers/).

With the markers in place, turn on **Guide** and the band will hear "Chorus,
1, 2, 3, 4" before each section. See [Voice guide](/docs/voice-guide/).

## 5. Separate what the audience hears from what the band hears

1. **Settings › Audio**: choose your interface and tick its outputs under
   *Hardware outputs* › **Apply**.
2. On the header of the **click** and **guide** tracks, set the in-ear output
   (for example *Out 3/4*); leave the music on **Master**.
3. Do the same with the output of the **metronome** and the **voice guide**,
   with the arrows next to *Click* and *Guide*.

See [Audio outputs and metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/).

## 6. Play it

Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> until you reach **Live View**: each section is a big
button. Tap it to jump to that part; when the jump happens you choose at the
top (right away, after a few bars or at the next marker). To stretch an
ending, **Vamp**. See [Live View](/docs/live-view/) and
[Live control](/docs/live-control-flow/).

Save with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>. LibreTracks also saves by itself every
few minutes.

## What next

- More songs: repeat step 2 with another folder; they are placed one after
  another.
- Another key or tempo: [Pitch, warp and the T button](/docs/pitch-and-warp/).
- Drive it with a pedal: [MIDI](/docs/tasks/midi/).
- From the band's phones: [Remote](/docs/remote-control/).
- Take it to another computer or to your phone: **File › Export session…**.
