A **pad** is a long, sustained bed of sound in one key, to fill in between
songs, under a prayer or while someone speaks. In LibreTracks the pad is not a
session track: it is a separate voice, like the metronome. It plays **even
without pressing Play**, doesn't depend on the song's master and keeps playing
when the song changes.

## Getting started

1. Press the **arrow** next to **Pads** in the top bar.
2. If you don't have any yet, open the **Pad manager…** and download a pack.
   Each pack ships all 12 keys.
3. Choose the pack and the key, and press **Pads** (or *Enable pad*) so it
   starts playing.

## The pads panel

![The pads panel](/guide/desktop-en/pads-popover.png)

- **Enable pad**: the same as the *Pads* button.
- **Pack**: the sound. Only fully downloaded packs and your own pads (marked
  *custom*) show up.
- **Follow song key**: the pad picks up the
  [key of the song](/docs/tasks/songs/#each-songs-settings) that is playing,
  even if you transpose it. If the pack doesn't have that key, it goes silent
  in that song.
- **Key**: with *Follow song key* off, you choose it, from C to B.
- **Volume**, in dB like the other faders.
- **Fade in** and **Fade out**: how long it takes to appear when you turn it
  on and to disappear when you turn it off. Without them it comes in and goes
  out almost at once.
- **Stop with playback**: the pad fades out when you stop or pause and comes
  back when you press Play. Off, the pad plays continuously between songs,
  which is the usual thing.
- **Output**: Master or a physical output of your interface. See
  [Audio outputs and metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/).
- **Pad manager…**: download packs and create your own.

When you change key or pack with the pad playing, the new one crossfades with
the previous one in a short fade, with no silence and without repeating the
attack at the start. If the new audio is missing, the previous one keeps
playing.

## Pad manager

![The pad manager](/guide/desktop-en/pad-manager.png)

- **Official packs**: **Download** or **Delete** each pack. They take up quite
  a lot of space; pads don't travel inside sessions or exported songs, so
  download them on every computer you are going to use.
- **My pads**: create a pad with your own audio. Give it a name and **Assign**
  a file to each key; keys without audio stay disabled. You can **Replace** or
  **Remove audio** on each one.

## From the Remote and automations

- The [Remote](/docs/remote-control/) has a pads widget to turn it on and
  change pack, key, volume and output from the phone. The packs are installed
  on the computer.
- An [automation](/docs/automation/) with the **Control pads** action saves a
  complete pad state (on, pack, key, volume and output) and applies it at an
  exact point in the song: changing texture in the bridge or getting the next
  song's pad ready.

## Before the show

- Download and test the packs beforehand; don't rely on a download during the
  show.
- Check the pad's output separately from the click and the guide.
- Leave some volume headroom: during the crossfade between two keys both play
  at once.
