**Compact View** shows the same project as the DAW view, but organised by
song: one column per song, with its clips, its play button and its volume, and
a mixer at the bottom. Whatever you change in one view shows up instantly in
the other.

It is the view for **rehearsing the setlist** and adjusting the mix without
getting lost in the timeline. To edit (move clips, place markers, change
tempos) use the DAW view.

Open it with the second button in the [toolbar](/docs/interface/toolbar/) or
with <kbd>Tab</kbd>, which cycles through the three views (DAW, Compact and
Live; <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> goes the other way).

![Compact View with its controls numbered](/guide/desktop-en/compact-view.png)

<Legend>

1. **Handle**: drag the song by it to move it to another place in the
   setlist. With the handle selected, the arrow keys move it one place at a
   time.
2. The song's **Play**: plays it right away if nothing is playing, or
   schedules the jump to it if another song is playing.
3. **Name**, BPM and key. Click to select the song; right-click for its menu.
4. The song's **Master**, with its meter.
5. The song's **clips**, in the order of its tracks.
6. **Column edge**: drag it to make the column wider or narrower.
7. **+ New song**: an empty song at the end of the setlist.
8. **Import .ltpkg**: adds an exported song.
9. **Mixer** shared by all the songs.

</Legend>

## Songs

- **Select**: click the column's header. The toolbar's transpose and warp
  controls now act on that song. The column that is playing also carries a
  coloured bar on its left.
- **Play**: with everything stopped, the column's play button starts that song
  from the top. If another one is playing, it schedules the jump with the
  [Song Transition](/docs/interface/toolbar/#song-transition) setting, just
  like <kbd>Shift</kbd>+number.
- **Menu** (right-click the header): rename, song key, change BPM, export the
  song and delete it. Deleting a song also deletes its clips and tempo
  changes; if it has clips, it asks you to confirm.
- **Reorder**: drag the song by its handle. It moves whole, with its audio,
  markers and tempo.

### Adding songs

- **+ New song**, after the columns: an empty song.
- **Import .ltpkg**: an exported song, added at the end.
- **Drag from your file manager**: an `.ltpkg` dropped anywhere on the song
  strip is imported at the end (a ghost column shows where it will land). A
  Reaper (`.rpp`) or Ableton (`.als`) project can be dropped here too.

### Dropping audio into a song

Drag audio from the [Library](/docs/tasks/library/) or your file manager onto
a song's column: each file creates its clip inside that song, and its own
track if needed. While you drag you see one slot per file.

Tracks created this way **delete themselves when they run out of clips**, so
trying things out doesn't leave empty tracks behind. Tracks you create
yourself are never deleted on their own.

## The mixer

![A compact mixer strip](/guide/desktop-en/compact-mixer.png)

<Legend>

1. Track or folder **name**. On folders, how many tracks it holds; unfold it
   to see their strips.
2. **M**, **S** and **T** (mute, solo and transposition).
3. **Pan**.
4. **Volume** in dB, with its meter.
5. **Audio output**.

</Legend>

It is the same mix as the DAW view's and the Remote's: any change shows up in
all three.

- **Only the active song's tracks**: the funnel button in the toolbar (it only
  appears in this view) hides the strips of tracks that have no clips in the
  song that is playing. Handy in sessions with many songs. Outside any song it
  hides nothing.
- **Select**: click a strip's name; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click and
  <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click for several. Whatever you touch on one applies to all
  of them, as in the DAW view.
- **Reorder**: drag a strip by its name. If you drop it in the middle of a
  folder, it goes inside.
- **Menu** (right-click): the same as a track's menu in the DAW view.

## On phone and tablet

Compact View is on mobile too, with **Mixer** to open the mixer at the
bottom. See [On phone and tablet](/docs/interface/mobile/#compact-view-and-live-view).
