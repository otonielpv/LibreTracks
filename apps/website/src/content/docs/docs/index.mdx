**LibreTracks** is a multitrack player for playing live with backing tracks:
you load the stems of your songs, add the click, the voice guide and the
section markers, send each thing to its own output (the music to the front
of house, the click to the in‑ears) and, at the show, jump from section to
section with a tap. It is free and open source, and runs on **Windows, macOS
and Linux**, on **iPhone and iPad** and on **Android**.

It is not a production tool: songs are recorded and mixed in your usual DAW
(Reaper, Ableton Live, Logic, Cubase…), and LibreTracks is what you use on
stage.

## What is in this guide

**The interface, button by button.** Numbered screenshots of every part of
the app: the [main screen](/docs/interface/main-screen/), the
[toolbar](/docs/interface/toolbar/), the
[ruler, tracks and their menus](/docs/interface/timeline/), the
[side bar and the Library](/docs/interface/side-nav-library/), the
[settings](/docs/interface/settings/) and what changes
[on a phone or tablet](/docs/interface/mobile/).

**Building the show.** How to do each thing:
[bring in audio](/docs/tasks/library/), create and arrange
[songs](/docs/tasks/songs/), place [markers](/docs/tasks/markers/), edit
[clips and tracks](/docs/tasks/clips-tracks/), set the
[tempo and time signature](/docs/tasks/tempo/), send [MIDI](/docs/tasks/midi/)
and project [video](/docs/tasks/video/).

**Live and audio outputs.** The [outputs and the metronome](/docs/audio-routing-metronome/),
[pitch and warp](/docs/pitch-and-warp/),
[jumps, Vamp and transitions](/docs/live-control-flow/), the
[Live View](/docs/live-view/) and the [Compact View](/docs/compact-view/), the
[voice guide](/docs/voice-guide/), the [ambient pads](/docs/ambient-pads/),
[automation](/docs/automation/) and the [Remote](/docs/remote-control/) to run
everything from a tablet.

**Sharing.** [Export and import](/docs/integration-ecosystem/) songs and
sessions, the cloud with your Google Drive, rendering to WAV and templates.

## Inside the app

The **Tutorial** button in the side bar opens guided tours that point at the
controls in the app itself: getting started, building a song, preparing for
the show and the three views. You can replay them whenever you like.

## If something does not work

See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting/). If it is not there, ask in
the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1795788251804551) or on
[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/LibreTracks/), or open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/otonielpv/LibreTracks/issues).
