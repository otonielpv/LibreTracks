The **Rhythm** panel adds two things for playing live over the song:

- **Loops**: a step drum machine (shaker, tambourine, hi-hat…) locked to the
  song's click. Every row has four variations (A-D), so you can change the
  tambourine in the chorus without touching the shaker.
- **One shots**: a grid of 12 pads in 4 banks to fire single effects (hits,
  risers, impacts).

They are not session tracks: they sound on their own, like the metronome, and
the song master does not touch them.

## Opening the panel

Press **Rhythm** in the top bar. It opens the panel of the view you are in:
docked at the bottom in DAW, Live and Compact, and as a tab in Setlist. On a
phone it docks at the bottom and keeps the transport visible. The button
lights up while a loop or a one shot is sounding, even with the panel closed.

![Rhythm panel](/guide/desktop/rhythm-panel.png)

## Loops

1. Pick a **pattern** in the list. The app ships 12 (pop, ballad, rock,
   worship 6/8, waltz, shuffle, dembow, cajón…).
2. Press **▶** to start and **■** to stop. Starting waits for the next bar
   (or beat, depending on quantize); stopping is immediate.
3. On each row: the name turns it on or off, and **A B C D** change its
   variation (on a phone, with a dropdown next to its steps). A requested change blinks until it comes in at the bar.

Header options:

- **Follow song / Free**: following the song, the steps land on the click's
  grid, also through tempo ramps and time signature changes. In *Free* the
  loop has its own tempo, which you set with **Tap**.
- **With the song**: the loop starts when you press Play and stops when the
  song stops. With it off, only its own button controls it.
- **Quantize**: when starts and variation changes come in: on the bar, on
  the beat or on the next step.
- Loop **volume**.

### A loop for each song

In the editor, **Save ▾ → Assign to this song** copies the pattern into the
song. When the song arrives, the loop switches on its first bar, and it
travels with the session when you export it (`.ltset`, `.ltpkg`), your own
sounds included.

## The pattern editor

Open it with **✎** in the panel header.

![Pattern editor](/guide/desktop/rhythm-editor.png)

- **Tap** a step to turn it on or off. **Hold** it (or right click) to change
  its intensity: soft, normal, accent.
- **Drag** to paint several steps in a row.
- **Variation A-D**: you edit that variation on every row.
- Bars (1, 2 or 4), time signature, subdivision (sixteenths or triplets),
  swing and the free mode's BPM. On a phone they are folded behind the
  settings button, so the whole grid fits.
- **▶** plays the pattern as it is, without saving it.
- **Save ▾**: to the library, to this song or as a new pattern.
- **Edit kit**: change the kit's sounds (up to 8), their color, volume and
  choke group.

## One shots

Tap a pad to fire it. With **✎** in the bank bar you enter edit mode: tapping
a pad opens its settings and dragging one onto another swaps them.

Each pad has a sample, name, color, volume, **mode** (*One shot* plays to the
end; *Hold* plays while you press it), **quantize** (immediate, beat or bar)
and **choke group**. **Stop all** silences them with a short fade.

## Your own sounds and packs

In the sample picker, **Import file…** adds a sound of yours (WAV, MP3,
FLAC…). Limits: 10 s for a loop sound and 30 s for a one shot. **Download
sounds** shows the free packs.

## MIDI and keyboard

Every control of the panel can be mapped in
[MIDI Map](/docs/live-control-flow/) mode: start the loop, turn rows on,
variations, pads (with their velocity; releasing the note releases a *Hold*
pad), banks and volumes. The controller's LEDs follow the state.

Default shortcuts: **Alt+L** starts or stops the loop and **Alt+1…4** put
every row on variation A-D. Pads are assigned in Settings → Shortcuts.
